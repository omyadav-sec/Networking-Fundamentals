# Types of Network — Day 2

## 1. PAN — Personal Area Network

PAN stands for Personal Area Network.

It is a network used to connect personal devices within a short range.

### Examples

- Smartphone
- Laptop
- Smartwatch
- Wireless headphones

### Real-World Example

A smartphone connected to wireless earbuds using Bluetooth is an example of a PAN.

### Where is it used?

PAN is commonly used for personal device communication over a short distance.

---

## 2. LAN — Local Area Network

LAN stands for Local Area Network.

It connects devices within a relatively small geographical area such as a home, office, school, or computer laboratory.

### Examples

- Home network
- Office network
- College computer lab

### Real-World Example

Multiple computers connected to a switch inside a college computer lab form a LAN.

### Where is it used?

LAN is commonly used in homes, offices, schools, and organizations.

---

## 3. MAN — Metropolitan Area Network

MAN stands for Metropolitan Area Network.

It connects multiple networks across a city or metropolitan area.

### Example

A network connecting multiple branches of an organization across the same city can be considered a MAN.

### Where is it used?

MAN can be used when communication is required across a larger geographical area than a LAN but smaller than a WAN.

---

## 4. WAN — Wide Area Network

WAN stands for Wide Area Network.

It connects networks across large geographical areas such as different cities, states, countries, or continents.

### Example

The Internet is the largest example of an interconnected global network.

### Where is it used?

WAN is commonly used to connect geographically distributed offices, branches, data centers, and networks.

---

## PAN vs LAN vs MAN vs WAN

| Network | Full Form | Coverage | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| PAN | Personal Area Network | Very small/personal area | Bluetooth devices |
| LAN | Local Area Network | Building/local area | Office network |
| MAN | Metropolitan Area Network | City/metropolitan area | City-wide network |
| WAN | Wide Area Network | Large geographical area | Internet |

# Interview Questions

## Q1. What is PAN?

**Answer:**

"PAN stands for Personal Area Network. It is used to connect personal devices within a short range, such as a smartphone connected to wireless headphones."

## Q2. What is LAN?

**Answer:**

"LAN stands for Local Area Network. It connects devices within a relatively small geographical area such as a home, office, or computer laboratory."

## Q3. What is MAN?

**Answer:**

"MAN stands for Metropolitan Area Network. It connects multiple networks across a city or metropolitan area."

## Q4. What is WAN?

**Answer:**

"WAN stands for Wide Area Network. It connects networks across large geographical areas such as different cities, countries, or continents."

## Q5. What is the difference between LAN and WAN?

**Answer:**

"The main difference is geographical coverage. LAN covers a relatively small area such as an office or building, while WAN covers a much larger geographical area and can connect networks across cities or countries."

## Q6. Which network is used for personal devices?

**Answer:**

"PAN, or Personal Area Network, is used to connect personal devices over a short range."
